Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

B-School Sim Lab (www.business-simulator.business) is a practice site for business simulations, cases and decision exercises. This page explains what we store when you sign in and how it is used.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address and, if you sign in with Google, your name and profile picture as Google shares them.
  • Your practice: the simulations and cases you start, the decisions and answers you enter, your scores and grades, and your settings such as your goal and weekly target.

We do not ask for or store passwords, payment details or your Google contacts, files or other Google data.

How we use it

  • To save your progress so you can continue on any device where you sign in.
  • To let the site owner see players' scores, grades and activity dates, to understand how the exercises are used and improve them. The site owner cannot see your written answers or drafts.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for advertising.

Where it is kept

Data is stored with Supabase, which hosts the site's database and sign-in. Sign-in emails are sent through Supabase, and Google sign-in is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy. If you use the site without signing in, progress stays in your browser only.

Your choices

  • You can download your history as a CSV from the Account page.
  • You can ask for your account and all of its data to be deleted, and it will be removed.

Contact

For questions or deletion requests, contact the site owner at the developer contact email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this site.
